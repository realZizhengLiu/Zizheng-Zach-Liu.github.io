I study an advertising auction in which consumers face uncertainty about product fit and incur costs to ask questions. Preliminary analysis indicates that a platform’s preferred assessment policy can diverge from the policy that maximizes consumer welfare, even when assessments are accurate and automatic provision is free. Assessments change both purchase decisions and advertisers’ willingness to pay, potentially shifting sponsored exposure.
