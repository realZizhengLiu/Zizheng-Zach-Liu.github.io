We develop a game-theoretic model of a UGC platform’s choice over the difficulty of cultivating fan loyalty. The analysis shows why content-centered and creator-centered strategies can coexist: supporting loyalty cultivation may reduce platform profitability under certain conditions. The results inform platform design and creators’ decisions about relationship-building and content quality.
