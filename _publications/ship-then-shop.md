Our two-period model captures how subscriptions and subsequent keep-or-return decisions generate data that improve matching accuracy. The tension between acquiring data and monetizing it gives rise to three pricing regimes: subscription fees only, product prices only, or both. The optimal regime depends on the cold-start threshold and learning capacity, while baseline matching probability and consumer churn further shape pricing incentives.
