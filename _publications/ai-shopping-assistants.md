The model allows detailed advice and general selling mechanisms. The ongoing analysis identifies an optimal design with at most two advice messages and one posted price per product. A cross-steering mechanism can direct some consumers away from their best-fit product in opposite directions across groups, shaping expected valuations and supporting more profitable pricing.
