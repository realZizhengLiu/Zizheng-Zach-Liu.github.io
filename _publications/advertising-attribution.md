We model a seller advertising through an e-commerce platform and a regular publisher. Within-outlet attribution tools can sometimes harm the platform while benefiting the publisher; an outlet with more effective ads may also engage in more aggressive misattribution. Allowing cross-outlet tools can create a win-win outcome for the platform and seller at the publisher’s expense, with implications for advertising measurement and market competition.
