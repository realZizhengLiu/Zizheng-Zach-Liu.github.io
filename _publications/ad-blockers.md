We develop a game-theoretic framework to study built-in ad blockers offered by platform publishers. A built-in blocker can facilitate coordination of ad displays between platform and content publishers while deterring excessive filtering by third-party blockers. Under certain conditions, this strategy can increase advertising intensity, benefiting publishers at the expense of consumer welfare.
